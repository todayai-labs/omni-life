Evaluation framework

When should the assistant speak?

Each episode starts with a standing task. The assistant uses the video and audio available so far to decide whether to intervene. Each required response has a target action, content and time window.

Dataset construction

How episodes are built

A real-world support need determines the task. The recording supplies the evidence for what the assistant should say and when it can say it.

Design the scenario

Specify who needs help, the everyday setting and what the device can observe.

Construct the video

Generate a controlled scene or select a real recording that fits the task.

Annotate timing and content

Mark response and silence intervals, then derive paired questions about what to say and when to say it.
